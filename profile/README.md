## Who we are

SolidStack is a founder-led software company based in Dubai Silicon Oasis. We take a project from the first idea to production and keep running it after launch. You get one team and one point of contact the whole way.

We serve clients across the **UAE, Iraq and the UK**, and we build products that work well in both **Arabic and English**, including right-to-left layouts.
